Out-of-fold

Train S1s are split into 5 groups by id. Each group is scored by a model that never saw it, so p1, p2, p_ce and p_v2 are honest inputs for the next stage.

Only provided data

No outside data or word lists; country only limits the search; all models MIT, largest 560M.

Models used: e5-small (118M) and e5-large (560M), MIT · our fine-tuned retriever and cross-encoder (from e5-small) · XGBoost for stages 1–3 · LightGBM for the auxiliary matcher. Libraries: PyTorch, sentence-transformers, transformers, polars, pyarrow, numpy, RapidFuzz, jellyfish, scikit-learn, anyascii.
